## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the tension between traditional bespoke luxury craftsmanship and contactless digital intelligence. Rooted in the tactile feel of heavy-ply matte cardstock, hand-brushed titanium, and debossed metallic foils, the visual posture communicates absolute authority, understated exclusivity, and frictionless utility.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
- **Dark Editorial Modernism:** Monolithic deep charcoal surfaces punctuated by atmospheric deep-forest tones and crisp, razor-sharp typography.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Elements simulate physical matter—matte carbon finishes, micro-etched textures, crisp metallic rim highlights, and subtle multi-stage light diffusion.
- **Controlled Restraint:** High density of negative space, rigorous micro-typography, precise hairline dividers, and deliberate, measured transitions replace superficial ornamentation.

### Emotional Target
- **Tactile Weight:** Evoking the quiet density of an ultra-heavy 600gsm cotton business card or CNC-milled black matte metal.
- **Technological Mastery:** Intuitive, instant, and frictionless digital tap-and-exchange interactions.
- **Private Reserve Exclusivity:** Curated for executive leaders, founders, and private wealth sectors who demand distinction without garishness.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an architectural dark profile. Background depths are driven by deep charcoals and subterranean forest blacks, producing rich optical contrast against warm bone-white type and metallic champagne accents.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Canvas & Structural Ground (`#171717` Charcoal Black):** The primary chassis. Used across app views, administrative dashboards, and the deep substrate of contactless card representations.
- **Deep Void Accent (`#06110C` Forest Green):** Sub-surface baseline used for nested surfaces, card backings, and subtle localized gradient vignettes to prevent monochromatic sterility.
- **Primary Contrast (`#F5F0EB` Warm Off-White):** Core foreground for high-priority typography, prominent cardholder titles, and critical system statuses. Replaces sterile blue-white to introduce organic warmth.
- **Secondary Emphatic (`#A31C1B` Crimson Red):** High-tension luxury accent. Used sparingly for critical alerts, primary card NFC sync indicators, active transmission states, and high-impact callouts.
- **Tertiary Metallic (`#C8B8AE` Soft Beige):** Simulates fine-gauge brass, champagne-gold foil stamping, and debossed metallic inlays. Used for badges, chip iconography, and key interactive outlines.
- **Muted Structural (`#A9A39D` Muted Text):** Secondary metadata, sub-labels, legal designations, inactive states, and unselected menu nodes.
- **Absolute White (`#FFFFFF` Pure White):** Reserved solely for micro-specular highlights, edge glints, and tactile reflection points.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between traditional bespoke print culture and clean digital interaction. 

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Editorial Statements):** Evokes legacy typography, serif precision, and high-fashion branding. Used exclusively for marquee headlines, hero introductions, executive names on card profiles, and statement statistics.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Interface, Body & Metadata):** A clean, highly legible grotesque sans-serif. Manages all functional UI aspects, body copy, form fields, card specifications, and dashboard analytics.
- **Micro-Caps & Labels:** All tokens assigned to `label-md` and `label-sm` must be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.14em` to `0.16em`) to echo mechanical serial numbering and foil-embossed authenticity seals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an airy, asymmetrical canvas designed to give physical card representations space to breathe. 

### Layout Model & Grid
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max container width of `1280px` centered with generous variable margins, minimum margin set to `3rem` (`48px`). Gutter width fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid presentation between `768px` and `1024px`, utilizing a minimum outer margin of `2rem` (`32px`) and `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid layout down to `320px`, with `1.25rem` (`20px`) side margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm enforces dramatic negative space; sections must never crowd adjacent content blocks.
- Spacing inside tactile cards remains condensed and disciplined (`space-md` to `space-lg`), while page containers embrace large breathing zones (`space-xl` and above) to emphasize high-end gallery framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use stark dropshadows or generic white glows. Depth is created through surface material changes, metallic specular borders, and dense, warm obsidian cast shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Ground):** Deepest level (`#171717`), fully light-absorbent.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card Bed & Substrates):** Matte obsidian (`#1D1D1D`), delineated by a 1px ghost border using `rgba(200, 184, 174, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating Physical Cards & Sheets):** Hybrid surface blending `#1F1F1F` to `#06110C` via a 145-degree angle. Accompanied by dual-plane cast shadows:
  - Ambient base: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.70)`
  - Direct specular penumbra: `0 2px 8px 0px rgba(200, 184, 174, 0.04)`
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Action Drawers, NFC Tap Prompts):** Elevated cards framed by a continuous `1px` metallic hairline border (`rgba(200, 184, 174, 0.25)`) and ultra-deep falloff shadow (`0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.90)`).

### Tactile Light & Specular Accents
Interactive cards leverage dynamic CSS linear-gradient light angles (`135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.08) 0%, transparent 60%`) overlaid with a 1px inner hairline stroke to replicate chamfered titanium edges.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is deliberate, sharp, and architectural. Soft pill corners are avoided in favor of subtle corner radii that match the standard dimensions of physical precision-cut CR80 ISO identity cards.

### Corner Treatment
- **Core Structural Radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used on inner actionable nodes, form inputs, segmented tabs, and data chips. Keeps components grounded and sharp.
- **Physical NFC Card Simulator (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Strictly reserved for digital representations of physical NFC cards, matching the standard credit card dimensional ratio (85.60 × 53.98 mm).
- **Modals & Flyouts (`0.75rem` / `12px`):** Max radius within the design system, used to cap larger floating surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Prestige Tap):** Solid `#F5F0EB` fill, `#171717` typography in `label-md`. Strict uppercase with wide tracking. On hover, background shifts softly to `#C8B8AE` with an instantaneous `1px` box-shadow inset glow.
- **Secondary Action (Foil Outline):** Transparent ground with a `1px` border of `#C8B8AE`. Text in `#F5F0EB`. Hover triggers an internal fill transition to `rgba(200, 184, 174, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive:** High-gloss Crimson Red (`#A31C1B`) used for direct NFC write operations or destructive account commands, paired with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Interactive NFC Digital Cards
- Aspect ratio fixed strictly to `1.586:1`.
- Built with multi-layered backgrounds: rich dark green-black gradient, topped with an authentic brushed foil watermark.
- Real-time physics-based micro-interactions: card tilts smoothly on cursor move (`perspective(1000px) rotateX(...) rotateY(...)`), dynamically shifting a simulated metallic light reflection across the debossed foil cardholder typography.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural slots: solid `#131313` background, framed by a `1px` border of `rgba(169, 163, 157, 0.25)`.
- Focus state switches the border to `#C8B8AE` with a localized crimson beacon dot at the right edge indicating live field engagement.
- Typography within fields uses `Plus Jakarta Sans` in `body-md` (`#F5F0EB`), with placeholder text set in `#A9A39D`.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact rectangular modules (`roundedness: 1`) utilizing an ultra-dark fill (`#06110C`) paired with a `1px` outline of `rgba(200, 184, 174, 0.3)`.
- Labeling strictly rendered in `label-sm` uppercase, communicating connectivity state (`NFC ACTIVE`, `ENCRYPTED`, `TITANIUM`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Minimal square and circular tokens with 1px border (`#C8B8AE`).
- Active state fills the interior with `#A31C1B` (Crimson Red) holding an absolute white center-dot micro checkmark.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Hairline-separated rows (`1px` border-bottom using `rgba(169, 163, 157, 0.12)`).
- Timestamps and analytical card tap counts set in monospaced or tracking-expanded `label-sm` (`#A9A39D`). Contact transfer notifications highlight the recipient's name in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`).
